# Publication boundary

This repository is a public projection of specific visual work samples only.

It intentionally excludes:

- private business structures or launch plans;
- customer, participant, or subscriber data;
- inbox content and correspondence;
- credentials, tokens, and connection strings;
- raw workflow reports and internal calibration notes;
- financial information;
- personal health information.

The public artifact state is `work_in_progress`. A public URL proves that the files are available; it does not prove that the audience understands them or that any commercial hypothesis is true.
